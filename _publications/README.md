# Academic Pages publications

Copy the 8 Markdown files into your website repository's `_publications/` directory.

Dates 2026-01-01 (Computing in Cardiology) and 2024-01-01 (AIME) are sorting placeholders because exact publication days were not verified.

The Computing in Cardiology entry was identified from a preliminary conference program, not confirmed final proceedings.

The oscillometric blood pressure paper was uploaded as a 2025 arXiv preprint and is marked as accepted for AMIA 2026; its file date is the preprint posting date.

The citation strings show the first author and highlight Masoud Nateghi. To show them on the publications listing, use `post.citation | markdownify` (not plain `post.citation`) in your site's template.
